Who we are

A preschool with heart, in the hills of Shillong

Since March 2012, Happy Feet has dedicated itself to nurturing children through their most formative years — building confidence, curiosity, and a genuine love of learning.

Our story

Happy Feet make Happy People

Happy Feet School is a distinguished private preschool situated in Pohkseh, Shillong. Since its inception in March 2012, our institution has been dedicated to nurturing children aged 3 and above. Our educational approach centres around fostering creativity and learning through play, while instilling positive social adaptability in our students across diverse circumstances.

Happy Feet transcends the realm of a mere Playschool. It is a secure, welcoming, and nurturing haven of curiosity where children are encouraged to flourish and evolve through engaging and developmentally fitting endeavours.

When our students complete their time with us, they are well-prepared for formal school — confidently embracing new experiences, with self-assurance, strong interpersonal skills, and a well-developed vocabulary.

Our philosophy

Learning that comes from doing

We firmly believe that every child has the potential to succeed when given the opportunity. We recognise that children's learning is enriched through firsthand encounters — so our curriculum is designed around exactly that.

🎨

Play-based learning

Art, music, storytelling, and hands-on exploration are woven into every school day — stimulating imagination and nurturing a genuine love of learning from the very start.

💪

Whole-child development

We develop gross motor skills, fine motor control, and sensory perception through purposeful activity — building the whole child, not just academic ability.

🤝

Social & emotional growth

We nurture resilience, emotional intelligence, and positive social adaptability — so children are ready for new experiences and the challenges ahead in formal school.

🏠

A safe second home

Happy Feet is designed to feel warm and secure. We actively address separation anxiety with patience and care, ensuring every child settles happily into school life.

📖

School readiness

Children leave Happy Feet with self-assurance, strong interpersonal skills, and a well-developed vocabulary — confident and fully prepared for Class 1 and beyond.

🔄

Current best practices

We stay current with evolving educational practices and trends, ensuring children receive a modern, thoughtful approach to early childhood education.

How to apply

The admissions process

Applications are reviewed on the basis of availability, age suitability, and appropriateness. Submission of an application does not guarantee admission.

1

Submit your online application

Fill in and submit the Nursery application form below. All admissions are processed online only — we do not accept walk-in applications.

2

Application reviewed

Our team reviews each application based on availability, age suitability, and appropriateness. Only the first 100 applications that meet the criteria will be considered.

3

Confirmation & enrolment

If your child is selected, you will receive a confirmation email. You will then complete the required enrolment procedures and receive the Happy Feet Handbook.

4

Welcome to Happy Feet!

Your child joins our warm, nurturing community — ready to explore, play, laugh, and grow with us every day.
